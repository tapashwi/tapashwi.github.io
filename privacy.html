Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This policy covers the Clear Explainer website (the "Site") and the Clear Explainer Publisher application (the "App").

1. The short version

We don't collect personal data from visitors, and the App doesn't touch anyone's account but our own.

2. What the App does

Clear Explainer Publisher is a first-party tool that uploads our own videos to our own social media accounts (including TikTok via the Content Posting API). It authenticates only against accounts we own. It does not request, receive, store, or process personal data of any other TikTok user or any third party.

3. Data we collect on the Site

The Site is static content. We do not run accounts, logins, comment systems, or tracking pixels. Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and operations; we do not use them to identify visitors.

4. Cookies

The Site does not set marketing or analytics cookies.

5. Third-party platforms

Our content appears on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. When you interact with it there, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data — we don't collect it in the first place.

7. Children

The Site is a general audience site and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date.
